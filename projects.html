Selected work

Case studies and scientific software.

Projects are organized around the computational question, method, evidence, and limitations—not chronology. Public examples are separated from unpublished trajectories and collaborator-owned research data.

Molecular dynamics case study

NaCl dissolution in water

An ordered nanoscale salt cluster is placed in explicit water and followed for 1 ns at 300 K. The case study combines real trajectory media with a connected-cluster measure to quantify progressive surface erosion.

Scientific software

Reusable computational workflows.

The strongest code examples are public repositories with clear scope, documented assumptions, reproducible environments, and automated checks where appropriate.

qctddft

Installable Python CLI for parsing Q-Chem TDDFT outputs, generating broadened spectra, detecting spectral regions, assigning states, and clustering representative structures.
